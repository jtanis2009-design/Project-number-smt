The basics

Light hits are fast. Heavy hits are slow and strong. A light hit that connects can go straight into a heavy or a special: that is a combo.

Blocking stops most damage. Some moves go through a block, and every super does.

The yellow bar at the bottom fills when you hit or get hit. When it is full, use your super.

Esc or P pauses. M turns the sound on and off.
